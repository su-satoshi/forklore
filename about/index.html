About

ForkLore is an independent tech blog exploring cybersecurity, AI vulnerabilities, blockchain cryptography, digital forensics and the hidden mechanics of online systems.

The name is a fork in two senses: the code you clone to see how something really works, and the folklore that grows up around technology when nobody checks. Posts here try to separate the two.

Who writes it

I'm Jaswanth, a Master of Cybersecurity student at RMIT. I write up what I learn from labs, CTFs, coursework and my own projects. Everything tested here is tested on systems I own or am authorised to test.
